About

Operational security thinking with strong support foundations

I am a cybersecurity and IT support professional with over five years of experience across technical support, systems administration, and security-focused roles. My background includes SIEM monitoring, security event analysis, incident tracking, reporting, and Data Loss Prevention operations in enterprise environments.

Through this work, I saw a common challenge in SOC operations: analysts often need to correlate alerts across multiple tools and systems to understand context and severity. That fragmented process can increase investigation time, create inconsistency in triage, and contribute to alert fatigue.

My formal SOC training and certifications, including CEH and ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity, strengthened my understanding of structured incident response, accountability, and alignment with governance frameworks. I bring a calm, methodical approach to security operations and technical problem-solving.
